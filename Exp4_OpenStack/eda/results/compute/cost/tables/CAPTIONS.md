- **budget_sweep_gemma4E2B** -- Score at matched BUDGET on Q1Q2, grader gemma4E2B. For each budget (the grid is every cumulative cost an arm actually reached) and each arm: its BEST checkpoint within that budget, chosen on sign_of(metric) * score, and its LAST checkpoint within it -- what a fixed-endpoint reading would quote. `frozen_after_peak` marks where those disagree. Scores are per-state means over the personas scored (Q1Q2); cost is cumulative GPU-hours to produce the state.
- **budget_verdicts_gemma4E2B** -- Who leads on Q1Q2 at each budget under grader gemma4E2B, by the best-within-budget rule and by the fixed-endpoint rule, with `rules_agree` marking the budgets where the two disagree -- which is where quoting a single endpoint would report a different winner. Never averaged across graders.
- **compute_by_arm** -- GPU-hours per arm from the trainer's append-only per-phase log (timing_sessions.jsonl), summed over every session. `build` is PTO-only -- GRPO computes its reward inside the training loop and has no preference-build phase. `resumed_iterations` > 0 means some iteration ran in several sessions (the cumulative sum here is still correct; per-process fields elsewhere are not). `unlogged_iterations` > 0 means a cost that cannot be recovered at all, so `gpu_hours` for that arm is an UNDERCOUNT and the per-iteration figure divides by the logged iterations only.
- **compute_by_iteration** -- One row per (arm, TRAINING iteration): hours by phase, summed over sessions. `iteration` is the training pass; `state_index = iteration - 1` is the model state it trained FROM. Join to a score frame on state_index, never on iteration. `resumed` (n_sessions > 1) marks iterations whose per-process timings elsewhere undercount; n_sessions == 0 means nothing was logged and the cost is lost.
- **compute_cost** -- Citable spend numbers. Every composite shows its arithmetic. Budget leaders are per grader and reported under both the best-within-budget and the fixed-endpoint rule, because the two can disagree.
- **cumulative_cost** -- Cumulative GPU-hours to PRODUCE each model state (state N is billed training iterations 1..N; state 0, the untrained base, is free). The conversations that MEASURE state N are generated in iteration N+1, so measurement is billed to the next iteration rather than to the state. `resumed_so_far` counts how many of the billed iterations ran in more than one session.
- **k_step_multiplier** -- Cost of look-ahead per TRAINING ITERATION, optimizer held fixed: median hours at the arm's highest K over median hours at its lowest, per phase and in total. Medians over the iterations actually logged (n_iter_* columns), so one resumed or straggling iteration cannot set the ratio. A multiplier of 1.9 means a K=5 iteration cost 1.9x a K=0 one for that optimizer.
- **method_step_multiplier** -- Cost of the METHOD per training iteration, K held fixed: median hours for PTO over median hours for GRPO (levels are alphabetical, so the ratio is PTO / GRPO). The `build` row is PTO's preference construction, which GRPO does not have -- its denominator is structurally zero, so that multiplier is undefined, not infinite. This is the cost side of RQ-ii: method/contrast matches on iteration, not on price.
- **timing_integrity** -- Iterations that need a caveat. RESUMED (n_sessions > 1): the cumulative per-phase sum used everywhere in this family is correct, but any per-PROCESS timing for that iteration is an undercount -- this is the Exp3 defect that made a 7.7 h iteration report 14,501 s. UNLOGGED (n_sessions == 0): nothing was recorded, so the cost is unrecoverable and every total below is short by it. An empty table is the good case.
