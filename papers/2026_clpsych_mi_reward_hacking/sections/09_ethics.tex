\section*{Ethics Statement}

\paragraph{No human subjects, no clinical data.} Every conversation analysed here is
synthetic: an LLM-simulated patient built from a persona specification, conversing with a
language model. No patient records, transcripts, or clinical data of any kind were used, and
no human participants were involved. The personas describe generic behaviour-change scenarios
and do not correspond to individuals.

\paragraph{These models must not be deployed.} The systems we train are 1B-parameter models
that, by our own measurements, end training more MI-inconsistent than they began, with
over-praise in a majority of therapist turns in one arm and open questions nearly absent.
Deploying such a model with people seeking help for substance use or other health behaviours
would be harmful. We report these as measurements of an optimisation procedure, not as
candidate systems. Nothing here should be read as evidence that automated MI is ready for
contact with patients.

\paragraph{Why the failure mode is worth publishing.} Sycophancy is a comparatively benign
failure in a chatbot and a serious one in counselling, where the therapeutic value lies
specifically in \emph{not} substituting the therapist's approval for the client's own
motivation. A model optimised for patient-reported satisfaction learns to be agreeable, and
agreeableness will score well on precisely the instruments a well-meaning developer would
reach for first. We think the useful contribution is the demonstration that this is
measurable cheaply and early, before deployment is contemplated.

\paragraph{Reward specification is a safety surface.} \S\ref{sec:learned} shows the drift is
traceable in part to items in a general-psychotherapy alliance instrument that pay for
therapist self-disclosure and direction --- behaviours MI does not prescribe. Reusing a
validated human-report scale as an automated training reward imports its construct
assumptions into the optimiser, where they are pursued far harder than any human respondent
would pursue them. We would encourage anyone building such a pipeline to audit the reward
item by item.

\paragraph{Compute and cost.} Training and evaluation used a single cloud A100 for the
training runs and API inference for the patient and graders, at ${\approx}\$317$ total API
spend. No proprietary or personal data was sent to any API; all transcripts are synthetic.
