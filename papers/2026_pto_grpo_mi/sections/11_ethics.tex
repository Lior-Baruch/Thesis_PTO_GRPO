\section*{Ethics Statement}

\textbf{No human subjects, and no clinical claim.} Every conversation in this work is between
two language models; no human patient data was collected or used, and no human participants were
involved. Nothing here is evidence that any of the four policies is safe or effective with
people. Motivational interviewing is a clinical skill practised with people in distress; a
1B-parameter model graded by another language model is a research artifact, and the gap between
the two is not bridged by any number in this paper.

\textbf{The documented failure mode is safety-relevant.} Both turn-level policies learned to
praise their patient more or less regardless of what was said, while their scores rose. In a
counselling setting, unearned affirmation of ambivalence or of a harmful plan is precisely the
error MI trains counsellors out of. We report it prominently, including the residual drift and
the channel relocation that survive under look-ahead, and would consider it a misuse of this
paper to cite either optimizer's gains without the behavioural results.

\textbf{Simulated patients encode a narrow population.} The 96 personas vary six attributes
around two presenting problems, were authored by the researchers rather than derived from
clinical data, and are a culturally narrow caricature of help-seeking; results should not be
read as generalising across problems, demographics, or cultures.

\textbf{Judges inherit their models' biases.} Both graders are commercial LLMs with undisclosed
training data; they disagree systematically about levels and, as \S\ref{sec:measurement} shows,
can disagree severely about individual conversations. Their use as arbiters of therapeutic
quality is a methodological convenience, not a validated measurement.

\textbf{Compute.} Training used single cloud A100-class GPUs for approximately
$8.1 + 19.7 + 27.9 + 51.2 \approx 107$ GPU-hours across the four arms, plus API inference for
the patient simulator and graders. The base model is openly licensed; the commercial APIs were
used within their terms of service.
