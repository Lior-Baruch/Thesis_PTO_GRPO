\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Optimizers for preference and reward learning.} DPO \citep{rafailov2023dpo} and its
descendants train on chosen/rejected pairs against a frozen reference; GRPO
\citep{shao2024deepseekmath} replaces PPO's learned value function \citep{schulman2017ppo,
ouyang2022instructgpt} with a group baseline over sampled siblings. The two families consume a
reward differently --- a pair versus a standardised group --- and comparisons between them are
usually run at a fixed reward design \citep[e.g.][]{xu2024dpovsppo}. Our question is the
complementary one: whether a change to the \emph{reward's evidence} lands the same way on the
two families. It does not.

\textbf{Look-ahead and search in preference learning.} Scoring a candidate by a simulated
continuation is the standard move in planning, imported into preference learning as MCTS-guided
search \citep{yu2023promptmcts, xie2024mctsdpo}, process supervision \citep{lightman2024letsverify},
and reranking against a reward model \citep{pace2024westofn}. \citet{baruch2025pto} introduced
the $K$-turn look-ahead lever for preference-tree optimisation of a motivational-interviewing
therapist, reporting gains in a cooperative, non-iterative regime; the present work embeds that
lever in a harder iterative regime and crosses it with the optimizer family. The companion
GRPO-only analysis of the two group-relative arms is reported separately; this paper owns the
$2 \times 2$.

\textbf{Reward hacking and LLM judges.} Optimising a proxy degrades the true objective
\citep{amodei2016concrete, skalse2022defining, pan2022effects, gao2023scaling}, and
LLM-as-judge evaluation \citep{zheng2023judging} adds sycophancy \citep{sharma2024sycophancy,
perez2023discovering}, self-preference \citep{panickssery2024selfpreference}, and length bias
\citep{singhal2024long, dubois2024lengthcontrolled}. We contribute a factorial handle on the
phenomenon: the \emph{horizon} of the reward selects whether the flattery exploit is reachable
at all, in both optimizer families, and the optimizer selects where the residual inconsistency
migrates. Our behavioural claims are corroborated by a deterministic transcript statistic rather
than a second model judge.

\textbf{Motivational interviewing.} MI \citep{miller1991mi} has established coding schemes
\citep{moyers2016miti} and validated session instruments \citep{hatcher2006waisr, larsen1979csq},
allowing the reward to be an instrument with published psychometrics; prior work has assessed
counselling quality automatically \citep{perezrosas2019goodcounselor, yosef2024assessing} and
simulated counselling with LLMs \citep{steenstra2025scaffolding, chen2025broaden}. We use MI
because the gap between locally-visible and downstream value --- the thing the horizon
manipulates --- is unusually explicit in its clinical literature, not to build a deployable
counsellor (see the Ethics Statement).
