\textbf{Endpoint.} At the matched iteration-10 endpoint, look-ahead leads on every instrument
under both graders (Table~\ref{tab:endpoint}). On the rewardrubric the gap is $+0.765$ (out of 1-5 Likert scale)
($\dz\,0.905$) under the training oracle and $+0.616$ ($\dz\,1.030$) under the held-out judge,
which reports the larger standardised effect[, and MI-inconsistent behaviour shows the
largest standardised effect of all - ??]. Against each arm's own base (indistinguishable: $2.963$ vs.\ $3.067$,
$\dz\,0.115$, n.s.), the \Kf\ gain on \QQ\ is $2.27\times$ the \Kz\ gain under the training oracle
($+1.554$ vs.\ $+0.686$) and $2.62\times$ under the held-out judge ($+1.038$ vs.\ $+0.396$);
anchored on the \Kz\ arm's \emph{best} checkpoint instead of its last, the ratios are
$1.53\times$ and $1.34\times$, so the gain is $1.3$ to $2.6$ times the turn-level gain.

\textbf{Onset, and the $\Kz$ decline.} For the first three iterations the two arms are
indistinguishable on \QQ\ under both graders (Figure~\ref{fig:headline}). The advantage opens at
iteration 4 and is Holm-significant at 6 of 10 iterations under each grader (training oracle: 4,
6--10; held-out: 4--7, 9, 10). It is not only $\Kf$ climbing: the $\Kz$ arm peaks at iteration 8
($4.082$) and falls to $3.753$ over its last two iterations, and under the held-out judge its
best state is iteration~3; against either best checkpoint the $\Kf$ endpoint still leads on \QQ\
under both graders ($\dz\,0.743$ / $0.384$ vs.\ iteration 8; $0.977$ / $0.386$ vs.\ iteration 3;
all Holm-significant).

\textbf{Replication of the endpoint.} Every [state -?] is scored on one 96-conversation sample, so we
re-drew the winning one (96 fresh conversations from the same $\Kf$ iteration-10 adapter, personas
and decoding, both graders): the two draws differ by at most $|\dz|\,0.174$ across 9 instruments
and both graders, nothing significant, and with the replicate substituted the endpoint gap on
\QQ\ is $\dz\,0.919$ / $0.949$ against $0.905$ / $1.030$. The re-draw bounds [evaluation noise,
not training variance -?] (see Limitations).
