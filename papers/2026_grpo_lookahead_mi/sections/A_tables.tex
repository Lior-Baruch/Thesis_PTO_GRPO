\textbf{The look-ahead rollouts.} In most cases the five-turn look-ahead ran as specified: of the
$121{,}088$ logged $\Kf$ candidates ($88\%$ of those scored), $82\%$ had a rollout that ran all
five turns, and most of the rest ended because the simulated patient closed the session ($16\%$
of all candidates). Candidates whose rollout ended early scored at or slightly below their
group's mean (by at most $0.09$ points).

\textbf{Where the lead comes from.} The 96 personas fall into three thirds by the cooperation the
simulator prompt sets (Table~\ref{tab:coop}). At iteration 10 the gap on \QQ\ under the training
oracle is concentrated in the two less cooperative thirds ($+0.96$ and $+1.13$); with cooperative
personas it is $+0.20$, not significant, as both runs score near the top of the scale there ($91\%$
and $100\%$ of conversations at 4.5 or above). Against $\Kz$'s iteration-8 checkpoint the
cooperative third is level under both judges, so the best-checkpoint lead comes entirely from the
other two. PCT follows the same pattern. MICI runs the other way: its gap is largest with
cooperative patients, whom the $\Kz$ policy praises most ($0.94$ over-praise acts per therapist turn
at iteration 10, against $0.48$ with uncooperative ones; at most $0.08$ under $\Kf$). On WAI-SR, the
$\Kf$ policy's gain over the Base is largest on the task and goal items ($+0.96$ and $+0.85$ under
the training oracle, against $+0.76$ on the bond) and the $\Kz$ policy's on the bond ($+0.63$,
against $+0.57$ and $+0.49$). At iteration 10 the $\Kf$ lead lies on task and goal (\dz\ $0.51$ and
$0.57$; held out $0.68$ and $0.53$) and is small or absent on the bond (\dz\ $0.26$; held out
$-0.06$).

\textbf{A test over the whole run.} To check that the iteration-10 contrast does not depend on the
endpoint, we averaged each persona's $\Kf - \Kz$ difference over iterations 1--10 and tested the 96
averages (Wilcoxon, Holm across the nine rows of Table~\ref{tab:endpoint}). Every row favors $\Kf$
under both judges at $p < .001$ (\QQ: \dz\ $0.92$, held out $1.13$; $|\dz|$ from $0.66$ to $1.04$
across the rows under the training oracle and from $0.54$ to $1.26$ under the held-out judge). Over
iterations 1--7 alone, MICI favors $\Kz$ under the training oracle (\dz\ $0.38$), as at iteration 3
in Figure~\ref{fig:headline}.

\textbf{The two Base draws.} The Base is two independent draws of 96 conversations from the same
model. Compared with the persona-paired tests of Table~\ref{tab:endpoint}, they differ by at most
\dz\ $0.13$ across its nine rows under the training oracle and $0.15$ under the held-out judge, and
by at most $0.18$ and $0.24$ across the twelve process measures of Table~\ref{tab:process}; no row
reaches $p < .05$ even before correction. This is the size of difference that sampling the
conversations alone produces.
