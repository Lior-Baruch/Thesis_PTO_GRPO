\caption{Per-channel $K$ contrast at the iteration-10 endpoint under the training oracle
(persona-paired \dz, $n{=}96$; positive means the $\Kz$ policy does more of the channel; hollow
bars did not clear Holm). Over-praise is the dominant channel by a wide margin, followed by
affirmations. The channels the $\Kf$ policy does more of are questions (the largest bar in that
direction), and, among the MI-inconsistent codes, direct/order and persuasion. Every channel is
reported so that substitution, MI-inconsistency moving to a different channel rather than falling,
is visible rather than hidden by the total.}

\caption{What the $K{=}5$ reward actually observed, over all $121{,}088$ scored candidates of the
run. \textbf{(a)} Share of rollouts that ended before five turns, by iteration (pooled $18.2\%$;
$15.6\%$ were the patient closing the session). \textbf{(b)} Reward relative to the group mean by
realised rollout length and ending: candidates whose rollout ended early score at or below their
group mean, by up to $0.09$ points when the therapist's own turn ended the rollout.
\textbf{(c)} Probability that a candidate is its group's best, by whether its rollout ran to
length (chance is $1/8$). The audit shows the lever was applied as specified in the large
majority of cases, and quantifies the mild keep-the-session-open pressure it carries.}
