\textbf{The look-ahead rollouts.} A check that the five-turn look-ahead ran as specified. A
rollout ends early when the patient closes the session or when the policy's own turn inside the
rollout ends it. Over the $121{,}088$ logged $\Kf$ candidates ($88\%$ of those scored), $82\%$ of
rollouts ran the full five turns; the early-ending share ranged from $12\%$ at iteration 10 to
$30\%$ at iteration 6, and in $16\%$ of candidates the patient closed the session. Early-ending
candidates scored at or below their group mean (by up to $0.09$ points, depending on how the
rollout ended) and were the group's best less often than chance ($0.10$ vs.\ $0.125$), so the
look-ahead reward slightly favours turns after which the session goes on.
