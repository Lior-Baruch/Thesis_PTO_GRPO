## Summary

The paper is careful and well checked. Every number the body quotes matches its appendix table, and the floats agree with each other. None of the false claims in settled decision 11 appears. Both ARR reviewer lenses land at Findings (3/3/3, confidence 4). The two main blockers are length and positioning. The body ends 34 lines into page 12, 346 column-lines over the limit. With the verified (smaller) estimates, the cuts that touch no settled decision reach only about 230 lines, so the length pass will also need Doron's sections and probably Table 2 or Figure 4 moving to the appendix. Second, the closest prior work is not positioned: RLFF-ESC (simulated futures inside GRPO for support dialogue) is uncited, CollabLLM already ablated its forward window, and MIThinker and SWEET-RL are misdescribed. A reviewer would read the method as incremental. Several sentences are wrong as written but cheap to fix: the 'lead is concentrated in uncooperative personas' sentence is false for MICI; the abstract turns a non-significant result into 'does not teach the MI response to sustain talk', and the held-out judge reverses it; 'gradients about twice as large in every iteration' is untrue for iterations 1-4; the persona prompts are credited to Yosef et al. although this study rewrote two cooperation clauses; and the Ethics statement contradicts the instruments appendix on praise. Beyond those, the main risks are causal, general wording ('teaches', 'It does', 'decided') resting on one run per K, and a patient-side story that rests on a simulator whose replies barely depend on the therapist and which sits inside the K=5 reward. One hedged sentence and prepared rebuttal material cover both. In lines_delta, positive numbers are body lines saved and negative numbers are lines added.
