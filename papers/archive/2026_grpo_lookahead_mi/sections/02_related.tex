\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Group-relative policy optimisation.} GRPO \citep{shao2024deepseekmath} replaces PPO's
learned value function \citep{schulman2017ppo, ouyang2022instructgpt} with a baseline computed
across a group of sampled completions for the same prompt --- attractive for dialogue, where a
value function would have to be learned over conversation states, but it concentrates all learning
signal in the \emph{relative} ranking of siblings, so the reward that produces the ranking is the
whole ballgame. Our contribution is orthogonal to the loss: we change what the reward observes and
leave the group-relative machinery untouched.

\textbf{Look-ahead and search in preference learning.} Scoring a candidate by a simulated
continuation is the standard move in planning, imported into preference learning as MCTS-guided
search \citep{yu2023promptmcts, xie2024mctsdpo}, process supervision \citep{lightman2024letsverify},
and reranking against a reward model \citep{pace2024westofn}. The immediate predecessor of this
work is preference-tree optimisation \citep{baruch2025pto}, which introduced the $K$-turn
look-ahead lever for MI dialogue with DPO \citep{rafailov2023dpo}. Untested, and tested here, is
whether the lever transfers to a group-relative optimizer --- and what it costs when the
continuation must be simulated by an API-served patient.

\textbf{Reward hacking and LLM judges.} That optimising a proxy degrades the true objective is a
long-standing concern \citep{amodei2016concrete, skalse2022defining, pan2022effects, gao2023scaling},
and LLM-as-judge evaluation \citep{zheng2023judging} adds sycophancy \citep{sharma2024sycophancy,
perez2023discovering}, self-preference \citep{panickssery2024selfpreference}, and length bias
\citep{singhal2024long, dubois2024lengthcontrolled}. Our over-praise result is a sycophancy finding
with an unusual handle: the exploit is \emph{selected} by the horizon of the reward, and we
corroborate it with a deterministic transcript statistic rather than a second model judge. The
one-sided grader saturation of \S\ref{sec:measurement} is, to our knowledge, not a documented
failure mode of judge-based evaluation.

\textbf{Motivational interviewing.} MI \citep{miller1991mi} has established coding schemes
\citep{moyers2016miti} and validated session instruments \citep{hatcher2006waisr, larsen1979csq},
so the reward can be an instrument with published psychometrics rather than an ad hoc rubric;
prior work has assessed counselling quality automatically \citep{perezrosas2019goodcounselor,
yosef2024assessing} and simulated counselling with LLMs \citep{steenstra2025scaffolding,
chen2025broaden}. We use MI not to build a deployable counsellor --- see the Ethics Statement ---
but because the mismatch between locally-visible and downstream value is unusually explicit in its
clinical literature.
