\section{Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}

\paragraph{Models and configuration.} Therapist: \texttt{meta-llama/Llama-3.2-1B}, bf16, LoRA
adapters ($r=16$, $\alpha=16$), learning rate $10^{-5}$, DPO $\beta=0.1$ (sigmoid loss),
preference margin $\tau=0.1$, trained on a single cloud A100. Client and reward oracle:
\texttt{gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18} throughout (client temperature $0.7$; oracle scoring at
temperature $0.1$, seed $42$, JSON-schema-constrained output). Held-out judge:
\texttt{claude-haiku-4-5}, structurally identical rubrics verified by an automated parity check,
submitted via batch inference. Candidate sampling at temperature $1.2$, $M=8$; therapist
conversation turns at temperature $0.9$, 200-token cap; 96 conversations per iteration; minimum
context 12 utterances; 49-utterance conversation target; 10 iterations $\times$ 2 epochs. Both
arms' full configurations are stamped in their run metadata, and the two differ only in the
look-ahead depth. Therapist \emph{decoding} is sampled and unseeded, so conversation sets are
not bit-reproducible; every claim is therefore statistical over the 96-persona draw, and the
measured same-policy noise floor (54 base-vs-base contrasts, none significant, max
$|\dz{}|=0.15$) bounds what a redraw could move.

\paragraph{Artifacts.} Every number in this paper is generated by a tracked analysis notebook and
written to a versioned artifact before being quoted; the paper's \texttt{NUMBERS.md} maps each
claim to the exact table or figure path it came from. The behaviour-channel contrasts come from
\texttt{7\_Stats} \S4d and the mechanism chain from \texttt{6\_Preference} \S5d, both rendered
under the \texttt{L5} view, which is the only view holding both look-ahead arms. Adapters for
both arms are archived on the Hugging Face Hub with per-iteration revisions.

\paragraph{Pairing.} Persona identity is \emph{recovered}, not read off file order: each iteration
reshuffles the same 96 personas under a per-iteration seed, so a file-order join across iterations
pairs unrelated conversations. Means survive that mistake; effect sizes and intervals do not.

\paragraph{Counts before rates.} Every per-turn rate in our instrument divides by the session's
therapist-turn count, which differs between the arms. All primary contrasts are therefore run on
raw per-session counts, with rates reported as secondary.
