\section*{Ethics Statement}

No human participants and no clinical data were involved: every conversation is between two
language models and every persona is synthetic; nothing was deployed to, or evaluated with,
people seeking care. The systems studied are research artifacts, not therapy tools, and the
result is a reason for caution: a therapist model trained against LLM-scored validated
questionnaires improves on those questionnaires while learning to flatter the patient, and the
lever we test replaces the flattery with unsolicited and directive advice (robustly in PTO; in
GRPO at intermediate iterations), the failure
Motivational Interviewing was formulated to avoid \citep{miller1991mi}. An evaluation reporting
only the reward would have missed it; LLM-scored clinical instruments should not be a training
signal without a behavioural audit alongside. Compute and API cost were modest ($8$--$28$
A100-hours per arm; \S\ref{sec:cost}, Appendix~\ref{app:tails}).
