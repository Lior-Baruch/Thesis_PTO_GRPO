Story: the partial-conversation Q1+Q2 reward at MCL=12 already ranks conversations like the full-conversation eval well above chance (0.86-0.89 sign-agreement, same grader) for all four arms; the ICLR motivation for look-ahead — that scoring K extra simulated turns makes the short reward a better proxy for the session outcome — is not supported at a fixed policy: on the matched-policy cut (train_iter 1, both K arms branching from the base policy) the K0-K5 delta is +0.004 [-0.067, 0.074] for PTO and +0.015 [-0.023, 0.057] for GRPO under the training oracle, and +0.007 / -0.014 (both n.s.) under the held-out judge. The pooled K5 lead seen when all iterations are mixed is a trajectory effect (PTO: K5 more faithful in 8/10 later iterations, iter-mean -0.022 [-0.039, -0.003]) or an iteration-range artefact (GRPO: on iters 1-5, K0 0.897 vs K5 0.890). Faithfulness is method-shaped instead: PTO's curve falls with trunk length while GRPO's rises, GRPO is more faithful within cooperation strata, and GRPO_LA0's cross-grader faithfulness collapses in its late hack iterations (0.63-0.70 vs 0.80-0.84 early) while its same-grader faithfulness barely moves — the reward keeps predicting the oracle's own verdict but not the held-out judge's, which is the reward-hack signature and connects to the held-out-instrument results.
