\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Trajectory-level reward and search-based preference construction.} Crediting a
dialogue turn by the continuation it leads to goes back to simulated-future rewards in neural
dialogue RL \citep{li2016deeprl}; it underlies look-ahead in preference-tree optimization
\citep{baruch2025pto}, MCTS-guided preference construction \citep{xie2024mctsdpo,yu2023promptmcts},
planning over simulated conversation futures \citep{chen2025broaden} and, one level down,
process supervision over reasoning steps \citep{lightman2024letsverify}. These methods are
evaluated on the reward that trained them, at post-hoc best or matched iterations, under one
optimizer; we test the lever itself under two loss families, priced, and read on the training
reward, on six further instruments, and under a judge that took no part in training.

\paragraph{Iterative self-improvement and group-relative optimization.} Regenerating training
data from the current policy each round is the common shape of self-rewarding and online
AI-feedback loops \citep{yuan2024selfrewarding,guo2024oaif}, best-of-$N$ distillation
\citep{pace2024westofn} and DPO on freshly sampled pairs \citep{rafailov2023dpo}; GRPO replaces
the learned value function with a group-standardized advantage over sibling completions
\citep{shao2024deepseekmath}. Our two arms are one such loop with two weighting rules --- best
and worst of eight above an absolute threshold, or all eight by group-relative advantage
(\S\ref{sec:mechanism}).

\paragraph{Reward hacking and judge biases.} That optimizing a proxy past a point degrades the
target is established in theory \citep{skalse2022defining}, for learned reward models
\citep{gao2023scaling} and across hand-specified rewards \citep{amodei2016concrete,pan2022effects}.
LLM judges prefer longer outputs \citep{singhal2024long,dubois2024lengthcontrolled,zheng2023judging},
agreeable ones \citep{sharma2024sycophancy} and their own family's text
\citep{panickssery2024selfpreference}. The hack our \Kz{} arms commit is the clinical form of the
sycophancy bias; \S\ref{sec:behaviour} shows the pressure look-ahead removes from one route
reappearing on another.

\paragraph{Motivational Interviewing, and two judges as train and test.} MI \citep{miller1991mi}
comes with a treatment integrity coding system \citep{moyers2016miti}; counselling quality is
separable from language alone \citep{perezrosas2019goodcounselor}; simulated patients train and
assess human counsellors \citep{steenstra2025scaffolding}; and \citet{yosef2024assessing}
validate the questionnaire-filling evaluators our reward is built from. We use MI fidelity
coding as the outcome of an RL comparison, which lets a relocation be told from a reduction. Agreement between LLM graders is usually treated as inter-rater reliability
\citep{zheng2023judging,koo2016icc}; ours cannot be, because the training oracle \emph{was} the
reward, so the second judge is a held-out split on the reward, never pooled with it, and their
disagreements are reported as findings about which claims are grader-invariant.
