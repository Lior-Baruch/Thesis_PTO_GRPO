- **judge_agreement_scatter** — Per-conversation scores, Claude Haiku 4.5 vs the primary oracle (gpt-4o-mini), one panel per metric, all model states pooled. Distance from the dashed identity line is level bias; scatter around a trend is rank disagreement.
- **judge_contrast_preservation** — K=0 endpoint contrasts under both judges (primary oracle gpt-4o-mini vs held-out Claude Haiku 4.5). Same-sign bars mean the result does not depend on the grader also having played the patient (Claude Haiku 4.5 is a different model family). MICI: lower = better.
- **multijudge_arm_means_dumbbell** — Arm means per model state (all four arms; GRPO K=5 ends at iteration 5) under the primary oracle (gpt-4o-mini) and Claude Haiku 4.5. Bar LENGTH is the level offset (large, and it cancels in every contrast); bar ORDER is what the thesis claims. Deliberately not averaged: the primary judge was the training reward and the second is held out, and the offset is model-dependent.
- **multijudge_concordance_curve** — Cross-judge ordering agreement (Claude Haiku 4.5 vs the primary oracle) vs effect size, per conversation pair. Shows how much per-conversation resolving power a given gap carries - i.e. why 96 conversations per arm are needed.
- **multijudge_gain_retention** — Gain retention under a held-out judge (Claude Haiku 4.5), all four arms' model states, with persona-bootstrap CIs. Uniform bars across arms = scale compression; one arm collapsing while others hold = that arm's gain did not transfer.
- **multijudge_retention_trajectory** — Gain retention vs iteration under Claude Haiku 4.5, one line per arm (GRPO K=5 ends at iteration 5), reference PTOExp3_LA0_Base. A line near 1.0 = gains a held-out judge also sees; a line declining with training = a policy progressively fitting the grader it was trained against, with the turn point estimating when that set in.
- **multijudge_retention_trajectory_Q1** — Gain retention vs iteration under Claude Haiku 4.5, Q1 only, all four arms — the single panel the retention claim rests on, sized for a one-column figure. Same data as the Q1 panel of multijudge_retention_trajectory.
- **multijudge_variance_decomposition** — Share of arm-mean variance by source (all judged model states, primary oracle gpt-4o-mini vs the held-out judge). A large judge-level slice is harmless (it cancels in contrasts); the arm x judge slice is the only component that threatens a claim.
- **oracle_repeatability_icc** — ICC(2,1) per model and metric from 3 re-scorings of the same conversations by the primary oracle (gpt-4o-mini); dotted = Koo & Li good (0.75) / excellent (0.90).
